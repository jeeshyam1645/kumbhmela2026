# Image credits

These files are the photos used for tent types that do not yet have a picture taken at this camp. Each one was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, resized so the long side is at most 1600 pixels, and converted to WebP. That resize is a change to the original, as the CC BY licences ask us to note.

The Swiss Cottage photograph, its poster frame, and the walkthrough video were supplied by Magh Mela Stays. They are not third-party stock.
